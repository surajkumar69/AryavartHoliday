📍 Cochin → Munnar → Thekkady → Alleppey → Cochin

Kerala 4 Nights / 5 Days Highlights

A classic tropical route covering lush Munnar tea hills, Thekkady spice sanctuaries, and overnight luxury Alleppey houseboat backwater cruises.

Day 1
Cochin → Munnar
Pick up from Cochin. Scenic drive passing Cheeyappara & Valara waterfalls to Munnar tea plantations.
Day 2
Munnar Sightseeing
Visit Eravikulam National Park, Tea Museum, Mattupetty Dam, Echo Point and Kundala Lake.
Day 3
Munnar → Thekkady
Drive to Thekkady spice country. Periyar Lake boat safari & elephant junction experience.
Day 4
Thekkady → Alleppey Houseboat
Board luxury houseboat in Alleppey. Cruise through serene canals with traditional Kerala meals.
Day 5
Alleppey → Cochin Departure
Disembark after breakfast. Visit Fort Kochi & Chinese Fishing Nets before drop-off at Cochin.

Kerala Stay Highlights

  • 🌿 Munnar Tea Resort Stay
  • 🐘 Periyar Spice Plantation Tour
  • ⛵ Alleppey Luxury Private Houseboat
  • 🚗 Private AC Vehicle Transfers
